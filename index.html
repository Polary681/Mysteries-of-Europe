Mysteries of Europe

Where the map ends, the legends begin.

Enter the ArchiveDiscover the legends

Europe is a mosaic of nearly fifty countries, languages and cultures. Beneath the cathedrals and capitals, every corner hides a story nobody has ever managed to explain: children with green skin, forests where time bends, music coming from empty houses.

This is our group project: a journey through maps, branching stories and 3D games to discover the darkest side of the continent. Choose where to start.
